Privacy Policy

Seekra Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data Collection

Seekra does not collect, store, or track any personal information. Your search queries are processed locally in your browser and immediately redirected to DuckDuckGo.

2. Search History

Search history is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through the settings panel.

3. Third-Party Services

Searches are redirected to DuckDuckGo, which has its own privacy policy. Seekra does not have access to your interactions with DuckDuckGo.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Seekra does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technologies. The only data stored is your local preferences (theme, font size, etc.).

5. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using your browser's private/incognito mode for additional privacy.

Seekra is a privacy front-end. Your searches are handled by DuckDuckGo according to their privacy practices.

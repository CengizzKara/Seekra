# Seekra: The Privacy-First Search Engine

## The Problem
In today's digital landscape, most search engines track and collect user data. Every search query, click, and interaction is logged, analyzed, and often sold to advertisers. This creates privacy concerns and exposes users to targeted advertising.

## The Solution
Seekra provides a beautiful, modern interface for privacy-focused searching. It acts as a front-end for DuckDuckGo, ensuring your searches remain private while offering a superior user experience.

## How Seekra Protects Your Privacy

### 1. No Data Collection
- No servers to collect your data
- No analytics or tracking
- No cookies for tracking purposes

### 2. Local Processing
- All searches processed in your browser
- Search history stored locally using localStorage
- Preferences saved on your device only

### 3. Direct Redirect
- Searches go directly to DuckDuckGo
- No intermediate servers
- No logging of search queries

### 4. Full Control
- Clear search history anytime
- Export/import preferences
- Choose your search engine
